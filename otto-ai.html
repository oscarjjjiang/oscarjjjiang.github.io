Attention Training

Health Management

True focus isn't about 'sitting still for long periods,' but finding balance between focus and health. Extended learning or work often leads to neck stiffness, eye fatigue, lack of exercise, and staying up late - these health risks ultimately undermine efficiency. The AI companion prioritizes health management as the second core function above work, not only reminding users to drink water, get up and move, and relax their eyes, but also proactively suggesting lunch breaks, evening relaxation, or sleep preparation based on focus duration and current time.
