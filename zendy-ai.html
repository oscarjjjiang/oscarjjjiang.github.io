Focus Mode

Based on the Pomodoro Technique (15-40 minutes adjustable), combined with computer vision to detect user behavior, providing instant feedback and point rewards.

  • Default 25 minutes, adjustable 15-40 minutes
  • Leave seat/phone detection → reminder and point deduction
  • 5 minutes → audio/light cues
  • 10 minutes → expression/voice encouragement
  • Complete Pomodoro → animation reward + points

Meditation Mode

  • Posture recognition (lotus/quiet sitting)
  • 10-15 minute countdown, guided meditation
  • Complete meditation → bonus points; interrupt → point deduction
